## 🎬 Killer Features (Differentiators)

### **1. AI Director Mode** 🔥🔥🔥
Input: Draft scene text
Output: Shot-by-shot breakdown, camera angles, visual mood, storyboard prompts

**Unique Value:** No other AI writing tool does cinematic preproduction.

### **2. Visual Scene Timeline**
Drag-and-drop timeline with tension curve overlay, character presence indicators, and one-click scene navigation.

### **3. Narrative Memory System**
AI tracks characters, locations, timeline, world rules automatically. Ensures story consistency across entire project.

### **4. Multi-Format Adaptation**
Prose → Screenplay, Poetry, Video Cards, Social Posts, Children's Books (one click).

## 🚫 What We're Avoiding

- Complex graph databases (use Firestore)
- Multi-agent orchestration (single Gemini pipeline)
- Enterprise features (focus on indie creators)
- Over-engineering (ship fast, iterate)
